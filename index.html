Whether you’re there for the music or ready to sit in, come as you are.

01

What to bring

Bring your instrument and a few tunes you know well. A lead sheet is helpful for a less-familiar tune. Just listening is welcome, too.

02

How sitting in works

Check in with Charlie when you arrive. Tell him your instrument and a tune you’d like to play; wait for the bandleader’s cue to join.
